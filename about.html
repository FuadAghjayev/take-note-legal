About Take Note

Book trusted specialists and local businesses in a few taps.

Take Note connects people who need a service with the specialists and businesses who provide it. Browse profiles, compare prices and schedules, book an appointment and keep every booking in one place — no phone calls, no back-and-forth messages.

Who it is for

UsersFind a specialist or business near you, see real prices and free slots, book instantly and get reminders before your appointment.
SpecialistsIndependent professionals publish their services, working hours and portfolio, and receive bookings directly from clients.
BusinessesSalons, studios, clinics and other venues manage their services, staff and appointments from a single account.

How it works

  1. Discover. Search by service, category or location and open a profile to see the description, price list, schedule, address, photos and reviews.
  2. Book. Pick a service and a free time slot. The specialist or business receives the request and confirms it.
  3. Show up. You get a reminder before the appointment. Payment, if any, is arranged directly with the provider — Take Note does not process payments.
  4. Share. Leave a review, follow the providers you like and see their latest posts and offers in your feed.

What we care about

  • Transparency — prices, schedules and addresses are published by the provider and visible before you book.
  • Trust — reviews come from real bookings, and any content can be reported in the app.
  • Privacy — we collect only what is needed to run the service and never sell personal data. See our Privacy Policy.
