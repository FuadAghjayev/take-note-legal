About Take Note

A simple daily notebook. Pick a date, write it down.

Take Note is a calendar-based notebook. Open the app, choose a day and write whatever matters — thoughts, plans, to-dos, memories. Every note stays attached to its date, so looking back is as easy as scrolling the calendar.

What you can do

Notes by dateEach day has its own page. Add as many notes as you like and find them again by simply tapping the date.
Calendar viewSee at a glance which days have notes, jump between months and revisit any day in the past or plan one in the future.
Edit anytimeChange, reorganise or delete notes whenever you want. Nothing is locked.
Private by designYour notes are stored on your device. We do not read them, sell them or use them for ads.

How it works

  1. Pick a day on the calendar — today, yesterday or any date you need.
  2. Write your note. Keep it short or write as much as you want.
  3. Come back later. Your notes are always there, organised by date.

Who it is for

Anyone who likes to keep a daily record: a journal, a work log, a study planner, a gratitude diary, or just a place to remember what happened on a given day. No account or setup is required to start.
