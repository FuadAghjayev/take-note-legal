Privacy Policy

Applies to the Take Note mobile application (iOS and Android).
Last updated: 19 September 2026

1. Overview

Take Note (“Take Note”, “we”, “us”) is a personal notebook app: you pick a date on the calendar and write notes for that day. Your notes are private. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is stored and what control you have over it.

Questions about this policy or your data: gulbeniz.m@gmail.com.

2. Your notes

Everything you write in Take Note — note text, the date it belongs to, and any titles, tags or attachments you add — is stored on your device. We do not read, analyse, sell or share the content of your notes, and we do not use it for advertising or to train any system.

If you use a backup or sync feature (for example your device’s cloud backup, or an account sign-in inside the app), a copy of your notes is stored in that service so you can restore them or open them on another device. Such copies are protected by the credentials of that service and remain private to you.

4. Permissions

  • Notifications (optional) — used only to remind you to write or review a note at the time you choose. You can turn reminders off in the app or in your device settings.
  • Camera / photos (optional, only if the app offers attachments) — accessed only when you choose to add a picture to a note. The app never scans or uploads your library in the background.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Information leaves your device only when you actively share a note (for example through the system share sheet), when you use a backup/sync service, or when a service provider processes data on our behalf (hosting, crash reporting). Providers act only under our instructions. We may disclose information if required by law.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Notes stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Deleting a note removes it immediately; uninstalling the app removes all local data.
  • If you have an account, you can delete it in the app (Settings → Delete account) or by emailing us. Account data and any synced notes are deleted within 30 days.
  • Diagnostic data, where collected, is kept for up to 12 months.

7. Security

Data stored on your device is protected by your device’s own security (passcode, biometrics, encryption). Any data transmitted to our servers is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Keep your device locked and your account credentials private.

8. Your rights

You can view, edit, export (share) and delete your notes directly in the app at any time. If you have an account, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of the data we hold about you, or withdraw consent, by contacting gulbeniz.m@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Children

Take Note is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows the current version; significant changes will be announced in the app.
