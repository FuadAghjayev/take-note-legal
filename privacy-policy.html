Privacy Policy

Applies to the Take Note mobile application (iOS and Android).
Last updated: 19 September 2026

1. Who we are

Take Note (“Take Note”, “we”, “us”) operates the Take Note mobile application, a platform where Users discover and book services offered by Specialists and Businesses, and where Specialists and Businesses present their services, publish posts and manage their bookings.

This policy explains what personal data we collect from each type of account, why we collect it, how long we keep it and what rights you have. For any question about this policy or about your data, contact us at takenote.support@gmail.com. We are the controller of the personal data described below.

2. Account types

  • User — a person who browses services, books appointments and interacts with content.
  • Specialist — an individual professional who offers services through the app.
  • Business — a company or venue that offers services through the app, possibly with several staff members.

The same privacy principles apply to all three; where the data we collect differs, this is stated below.

3. What we collect and why

3.1 Account and profile information

To create an account you provide a name, surname and mobile phone number, which we verify with a one-time code (OTP). Specialists and Businesses additionally provide an email address and professional profile details. You may also add a profile photo and other optional profile fields.

We use this information for registration, authentication, creating and managing your account, contacting you about your bookings, and providing the Take Note services and app functionality. Optional fields can be left empty or removed at any time in your profile.

3.2 Service listings (Specialists and Businesses)

Specialists and Businesses publish service listings containing the service name, description, price, working schedule, address, photos and other service details. This information is presented to Users who wish to find and book those services and is therefore visible to all Users of the app. Do not include personal data you do not want to make public in a listing.

3.3 Bookings and appointments

When a User books a service we process the booking details: the selected service, date and time, the Specialist or Business involved, the status of the booking and any note the User adds. Booking details are shared between the User and the Specialist or Business concerned so that the appointment can be fulfilled.

3.4 Content you publish

Users, Specialists and Businesses may publish posts, photos, text, reviews and other content in the app. Published content is visible to other users of the app together with your display name and profile photo. We use this content to operate the app and provide its features (feeds, profiles, search, ratings). You can edit or delete your own content at any time; deleted content is removed from the app and from our active systems within 30 days.

We reserve the right to remove content that violates our terms of use or applicable law, and users can report content directly in the app.

3.5 Camera and photos

With your permission, the app accesses your camera or photo library only when you choose to add a photo to your profile, a listing or a post. We do not scan or upload your library in the background.

3.6 Location

If you grant location permission, we use your device’s location to show Specialists and Businesses near you and to sort results by distance. Location is read only while you are using the app. You can decline or withdraw this permission in your device settings; the app remains usable, but distance-based features will not work. Business and Specialist addresses entered in listings are stored as part of the listing.

3.7 Notifications

With your permission we send push notifications about booking confirmations, reminders, changes and cancellations, and about activity related to your account. You can turn notifications off in the app settings or in your device settings at any time. Notification delivery uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google LLC), which receives a device token but no content of your profile or bookings.

3.8 Technical and usage data

When you use the app we automatically receive technical data such as device model, operating system version, app version, language, IP address, and diagnostic information about crashes and errors. We use this data to keep the app secure, prevent abuse and fix problems. We do not use advertising identifiers and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

5. Legal basis

We process your data to perform our contract with you (providing the app, creating your account, fulfilling bookings), on the basis of your consent (optional permissions such as location, camera and notifications, which you can withdraw at any time), and for our legitimate interests in keeping the app secure and improving it, where these are not overridden by your rights.

6. Who we share data with

  • Other users of the app — the information marked “visible” in the table above, and booking details between the parties of a booking.
  • Service providers acting on our behalf — hosting and infrastructure, an SMS provider used only to deliver one-time codes, and Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications. These providers process data only under our instructions.
  • Authorities — where required by law or to protect the rights, safety or property of Take Note or its users.

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

7. Payments

Take Note does not process payments inside the app and does not collect card or bank details. Payment for a service, if any, is arranged directly between the User and the Specialist or Business.

8. How long we keep your data

  • Account and profile data — for as long as your account exists.
  • Service listings, posts and reviews — until you delete them or your account is deleted.
  • Booking records — for the lifetime of the account and, in anonymised form, for statistics afterwards.
  • Technical logs and diagnostics — up to 12 months.

When you delete your account, personal data is removed or anonymised within 30 days, except where we must retain it to comply with a legal obligation or to resolve a dispute.

9. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored on access-controlled servers. Authentication tokens are stored in the device’s secure storage. No system is completely secure; please keep your phone locked and report any suspected misuse to us immediately.

10. Your rights

You can access and edit most of your data directly in the app (Profile). In addition you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent, and to receive a copy of the data you provided. To exercise these rights contact us at takenote.support@gmail.com; we respond within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

11. Account deletion

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Delete account. After confirmation, your account, profile, listings, posts and bookings are deleted as described in section 8. If you cannot access the app, email us and we will delete the account for you after verifying your identity.

12. Children

Take Note is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

13. International transfers

Our servers and service providers may be located outside your country. Where data is transferred internationally we rely on appropriate safeguards such as contractual protections with our providers.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows the current version. For significant changes we will notify you in the app before they take effect.
